LatestJuly 2026

Fundraising begins for 1,000 backpack sets

We've reached an exciting milestone: with the backpack and coloring book designs complete, Pack with Love is beginning to raise support for our founding-year commitment — 1,000 complete backpack sets for children across ten villages in Senegal, funded by one $5,300 shared goal.

Every gift helps a child open a backpack made just for them. Thank you for being part of the very first chapter of our story.
