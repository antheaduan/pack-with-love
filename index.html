What we make

A backpack two children
finish together

Our signature gift starts as a theme design drawn by a student here — and becomes complete only when a child there colors it in and makes it their own. Something useful to carry. Something joyful to create. A message that stays.

1.

A kid designs it

Our student designers create the theme art — backpack panels and matching coloring books. drawn with love in California

2.

A kid colors it

The child who receives it picks up the crayons and finishes the creation — their colors, their backpack, theirs to keep. completed with joy in Senegal

3.

The message stays

Inside every set: a coloring book and words of encouragement that remind each child they are welcomed and valued. you are loved. you matter.

Who we are

Run by kids.
Guarded by grown-ups.

Pack with Love was founded by Joanna Zhang as a sixth-grader, with one desire: that every child would know they are loved, seen, and remembered. Students lead in age-appropriate ways — vision, design, outreach, encouragement, and impact storytelling.

Behind them stands a team of Adult Advisors and mentors — educators, a university professor, entrepreneurs, and a physician — who hold the responsibilities that should never rest on a child: money, contracts, safety, and partner commitments.

“Adult Advisors build the guardrails; student leaders carry the vision forward.”

The guardrails, plainly
  • Money: all funds move through authorized adult-managed channels — students never collect, hold, or manage project money.
  • Safety: every in-person activity is supervised by an Adult Advisor; parent permission comes first.
  • Privacy: children's photos, names, and stories are shared only with partner approval and safeguarding rules.
  • Giving: no child is ever required to attend anything or share anything to receive a backpack.
