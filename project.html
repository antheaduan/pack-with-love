The purpose

Complete backpack sets, packed with love

Our founding project is delivered in partnership with Africa Cries Out (ACO), a Christ-centered nonprofit bringing medical care, hope, and compassionate service to communities in Senegal — over 30,000 patients served, 10+ annual missions, and 100+ volunteers mobilized each year.

It supports ACO's Children's Hope & Care program in Senegal. Complete, child-centered backpack sets will be packed at the ACO base and shared with children across about ten villages, traveling with the March 2027 ACO Medical Mission.

The founding-year commitment: 1,000 backpack sets — and once those are funded and shipped, anything raised beyond them supports the wider work of Children's Hope & Care: the children's side of each ACO mission, where kids get Bible stories, songs, games, and crafts in a safe space while their families are seen at the clinic.

One principle holds above all: every backpack is a gift, unconditional. No child is ever required to attend an activity or share anything to receive one.

Inside every backpack

The four core items

These four items are the main project. They're designed to belong together as one thoughtful gift.

  • 1
    Backpack
    A child-friendly backpack with a theme-based coloring design each child can personalize.
  • 2
    Coloring Book
    A matching book that connects to the backpack's artwork with Bible truths and encouragement.
  • 3
    Gospel Wordless Book
    A simple, colorful book that shares the good news of Jesus with colors instead of words.
  • 4
    Coloring Set
    The crayons or coloring tools that bring the backpack and book to life.

Optional extras — school supplies, handmade cards, bookmarks, and more — may be added separately, as long as they never delay the four core items.

More than an activity book

The coloring book

Each coloring book has two sections, designed to help a child feel seen, loved, and connected.

Section One — The Gospel Wordless Book. A simple, colorful way to share the good news of Jesus with colors instead of words — so it crosses every language barrier. Each color tells part of the story:

  • Gold · Heaven
  • Dark · Our sin
  • Red · Jesus' love
  • White · A clean heart
  • Green · Growing with Jesus

Section Two — Children's Hearts to Hearts. Pages created by our young designers to share joy and encouragement, child to child: “I made this for you. I hope it brings you joy.”

How the year unfolds

The year in three acts

Design the gift, raise the means, and deliver it in person. A participant can join for one act — or all three.

Act 1 — Design & Give

Before anything can be funded or shipped, it has to be imagined. Our student design team creates the gift itself — the color-it-yourself backpack panels and matching coloring books — so the child who receives the backpack finishes the creation the child who designed it began. Idea → design → produce → ship → give.

Act 2 — Raise

One shared goal, three ways in: give directly, claim a portion and carry it yourself, or join the Data to Impact course and carry it as a team. Every rung uses the same Four Moves — what changes is depth.

Act 3 — Deliver

The loop can close in person. Club members and families may join the March 2027 ACO Medical Mission to Senegal — packing sets at the ACO base and placing them, one by one, into the hands they were made for. Optional, and registered separately through ACO's mission program.

Done responsibly

One project, one number, one truth

Every dollar from every rung — direct gifts, individual claims, the class's team claim — lands on the same $5,300 shared goal and is recorded the moment it arrives, on the contributor's own progress and the shared total together.

All donations are received and managed through ACO's approved financial channels. Students help with planning, design, and communication — but never personally collect, hold, or manage funds. Children's personal fundraising pages show first names and general grade level only. And every backpack is a gift, unconditional — no child is ever required to attend an activity or share anything to receive one.

If gifts run past $5,300, nothing is returned and nothing leaves the purpose you gave for: once the 1,000 sets are funded, produced, and shipped, additional gifts support the wider work of ACO's Children's Hope & Care program — the part of each mission that places these backpacks in children's hands.

Donations are received by Africa Cries Out, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN: 81-3022255), and are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Pack with Love does not itself hold or manage funds.

Before you give

Questions we get asked

What exactly does the $5,300 pay for?

$4,500 covers 1,000 complete backpack sets at $4.50 each — a backpack, a coloring book, a Gospel Wordless Book, and a coloring set. The remaining $800 covers ocean shipping, import duties, and customs clearance, based on ACO's actual shipping history and a confirmed quote.

There is one goal and one number. Every gift — a direct donation, a child's bake sale, the math class's sales events — lands on that same $5,300.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Donations are received by Africa Cries Out, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN: 81-3022255), and are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. ACO issues the receipt.

Pack with Love does not itself hold or manage funds. Students help with planning, design, and communication — they never personally collect or hold money.

Who receives the backpacks, and when?

Children across roughly ten villages in Senegal, through ACO's Children's Hope & Care program — a partner already trusted in those communities, not a new operation we are inventing.

The sets travel with the March 2027 ACO Medical Mission. That deadline drives the whole calendar: everything has to be funded, produced, ocean-shipped, and cleared through customs before it departs.

What happens if you raise more than $5,300?

Nothing is returned, and nothing is redirected outside the work you gave toward. The 1,000 sets get funded, produced, ocean-shipped, and cleared through customs first — that is what the goal is for.

Gifts beyond it support the wider work of ACO's Children's Hope & Care program — the children's part of each mission, where these backpacks are handed out alongside Bible stories, songs, games, and crafts while families are seen at the clinic. We would rather tell you that now than surprise you with it later.

Does a child have to do anything to receive a backpack?

No. Every backpack is a gift, unconditional. No child is ever required to attend an activity, believe anything, or share anything in order to receive one.

The same holds on our side: a child who claims part of the goal is committing to effort, not to a debt. If a claim turns out to be bigger than the year allows, it gets resized — that is a normal checkpoint, not a failure.

Still have a question? Ask us directly — a real person answers.
