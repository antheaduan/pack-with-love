The Love & Hope Backpack Project

1,000 backpack sets for children across ten villages in Senegal · one $5,300 shared goal · traveling with the March 2027 ACO Medical Mission.

Purpose Goal Ways In Calendar Accountability
The purpose

Complete backpack sets, packed with love

Our founding project supports ACO's Children's Hope & Care program in Senegal. Complete, child-centered backpack sets will be packed at the ACO base and shared with children across about ten villages, traveling with the March 2027 ACO Medical Mission.

We deliver it in partnership with Africa Cries Out (ACO), a Christ-centered nonprofit bringing medical care, hope, and compassionate service to communities in Senegal — over 30,000 patients served, 10+ annual missions, and 100+ volunteers mobilized each year.

The founding-year commitment: 1,000 backpack sets. And one principle above all: every backpack is a gift, unconditional. No child is ever required to attend an activity or share anything to receive one.

How the year unfolds

The year in three acts

Design the gift, raise the means, and deliver it in person. A participant can join for one act — or all three.

Act 1 — Design & Give

Before anything can be funded or shipped, it has to be imagined. Our student design team creates the gift itself — the color-it-yourself backpack panels and matching coloring books — so the child who receives the backpack finishes the creation the child who designed it began. Idea → design → produce → ship → give.

Act 2 — Raise

One shared goal, three ways in: give directly, claim a portion and carry it yourself, or join the Data to Impact course and carry it as a team. Every rung uses the same Four Moves — what changes is depth.

Act 3 — Deliver

The loop can close in person. Club members and families may join the March 2027 ACO Medical Mission to Senegal — packing sets at the ACO base and placing them, one by one, into the hands they were made for. Optional, and registered separately through ACO's mission program.

Act 2 · Raise

Three ways in

A ladder that welcomes a one-time donor, a child with a Saturday bake sale, and a student ready for a 24-session course (September–March). Every rung raises money for the same shared goal.

1 · Give

Anyone. Any amount. Family, friends, and community members donate directly toward the $5,300 goal. No claim, no program — just generosity, fully counted.

The heart of every set

The four core items

These four items are the main project. They're designed to belong together as one thoughtful gift.

  • 1
    Backpack
    A child-friendly backpack with a theme-based coloring design each child can personalize.
  • 2
    Coloring Book
    A matching book that connects to the backpack's artwork with Bible truths and encouragement.
  • 3
    Gospel Wordless Book
    A simple, colorful book that shares the good news of Jesus with colors instead of words.
  • 4
    Coloring Set
    The crayons or coloring tools that bring the backpack and book to life.

Optional extras — school supplies, handmade cards, bookmarks, and more — may be added separately, as long as they never delay the four core items.

More than an activity book

The coloring book

Each coloring book has two sections, designed to help a child feel seen, loved, and connected.

Section One — The Gospel Wordless Book. A simple, colorful way to share the good news of Jesus with colors instead of words — so it crosses every language barrier. Each color tells part of the story:

  • Gold · Heaven
  • Dark · Our sin
  • Red · Jesus' love
  • White · A clean heart
  • Green · Growing with Jesus

Section Two — Children's Hearts to Hearts. Pages created by our young designers to share joy and encouragement, child to child: “I made this for you. I hope it brings you joy.”

Done responsibly

One project, one number, one truth

Every dollar from every rung — direct gifts, individual claims, the class's team claim, employer matches — lands on the same $5,300 shared goal and is recorded the moment it arrives, on the contributor's own progress and the shared total together.

All donations are received and managed through ACO's approved financial channels. Students help with planning, design, and communication — but never personally collect, hold, or manage funds. Children's personal fundraising pages show first names and general grade level only. And every backpack is a gift, unconditional — no child is ever required to attend an activity or share anything to receive one.

Donations are received by Africa Cries Out, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN: 81-3022255), and are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Pack with Love does not itself hold or manage funds.
