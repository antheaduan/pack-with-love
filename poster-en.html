Why statistics? AI can compute every answer in an instant — but it cannot judge whether the data can be trusted. Asking good questions, sampling, spotting bias, deciding on evidence — statistical thinking is the most durable math of the AI era, and the most compressed chapter in the middle-school curriculum. This course teaches it as your child's own business.

THE ACTUAL MATHWhat math do they learn?

  • Fractions · percentages · ratios — turning the class's real survey data into frequency tables and conclusions
  • Sampling & bias — random vs. stratified; why asking only your friends gives you wrong data
  • Weighted averages & scoring models — choosing a supplier from two real quotes, with math
  • Cost equations & break-even — solving "how many must we sell?"; real tariffs in the landed cost
  • Charts & distributions — bar charts, side-by-side comparisons; reading a market in ten seconds
  • Prediction & error — forecast sales, then compute percent error against real results

WHAT THEY WALK AWAY WITHWhat does your child get?

  • A portfolio of 24 real business artifacts — no exams; the work is the assessment
  • A professor + entrepreneurs teaching — an accounting professor (CFA) and two industry veterans with 20–30 years in trade & manufacturing
  • Real sales events — real customers, real pricing, real results
  • The three things AI can't take — statistics teaches judgment, business teaches responsibility, service gives meaning
  • Automatic club membership — Pack with Love, free forever

Why does the profit become backpacks?

Pack with Love is a youth service club founded by Bay Area students, with adult advisors holding the guardrails. This course is part of the club's first project, the Love & Hope Backpack Project: the class's squishy profits join a shared $5,300 goal — alongside community gifts and kids' own fundraising — sending 1,000 color-it-yourself backpack sets to children across ten villages in Senegal, traveling with the March 2027 medical mission and delivered in person.

Every teacher volunteers. Tuition covers materials & operations — the surplus also goes to the project. Sign-up is free and reserves your seat — tuition due only once the course is confirmed (6+).

Your child learns real skills; a child far away gets a backpack — win-win.
