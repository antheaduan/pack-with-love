1 · Give

Anyone. Any amount. Family, friends, and community members donate directly toward the $5,300 goal. No claim, no program — just generosity, fully counted.

One framework for everyone

The Four Moves

Every participant — one child with a lemonade stand or a whole class with a data project — carries their claim through the same four moves. Every dollar counts twice at once: on their own progress bar and on the shared goal.

Claim

Decide what's yours. Take an honest inventory — a talent, a bake sale, people you know — and size a claim to match. Every claim is confirmed by a parent.

Plan

Turn the claim into a working plan, ending with three artifacts: the ask, the schedule, and the tracking sheet.

Do

Ask, hear no, keep going. Real asks, face to face. Track every dollar, reconcile weekly, and revise the plan rather than abandon it.

Report

Close the loop: a final account, a personal thank-you to every supporter, and one impact statement — “Your trust became ___ backpacks.”

RESIZE — the midpoint checkpoint. Halfway through, every participant asks honestly: ahead, on track, or behind — and why? Ahead may claim more; behind may resize down, releasing the rest to the shared pool. Resizing down is not failing — it's Move 1 done again with better information.
The project clock

One year, one calendar

Every family sees the same calendar. All dates are approximate — the real project comes first.

  • ≈ Mid – late Sept 2026

    Move 1 — Claim

    Claims registered & parent-confirmed.

  • ≈ Late Sept – mid Oct 2026

    Move 2 — Plan

    The ask, the schedule, the tracking sheet.

  • ≈ Mid Oct 2026 – Feb 28, 2027

    Move 3 — Do

    The full season — including Sales Events I & II in January — with weekly reconciliation.

  • ≈ Mid Dec 2026

    Resize checkpoint

    Claims resized or confirmed before the holiday-season push.

  • ≈ Nov – Dec 2026

    Production order placed

    Funded by the bridge advance, per the supplier's confirmed lead time; ocean shipping takes 5–6 weeks.

  • ≈ March 1, 2027

    Funds close

    All money in; the $5,300 bar is final.

  • ≈ March 1 – 6, 2027

    Move 4 — Report

    Final accounts, thank-yous, impact statements.

  • March 7, 2027

    Final Impact Showcase

    “Your trust became ___ backpacks.” — the final number, presented.

Two clocks, one bridge

This project runs on two clocks. The production clock is fixed by the mission: backpacks must be ordered, shipped, and cleared before March 2027 — so production never waits on the progress bar. It is guaranteed by a bridge advance from the founding family, paid to the supplier on schedule.

The fundraising clock runs the full season, so the January sales events, the holiday season, and every late gift all count. Donations repay the bridge as they arrive; whatever remains uncovered at close becomes the founding family's own gift to the project.

No child owes the gap. A claim is a commitment to effort, not a debt.
