Who We Are

An independent, youth-led service club where creativity, teamwork, and compassion become real care for children and communities.

Our story

It began with a question

Pack with Love began with stories. Founder Joanna Zhang grew up hearing her mom share experiences from mission trips to Africa — and three of them stayed with her, and slowly became the beginning of Pack with Love.

In the jungle of Djindjin

Many children walk hours to school each day. There's no lunch, and the long walk home leaves them hungry until dinner. Asked what they needed most, they said “food” — which ACO already sends each year. Asked what else, they answered: “a backpack,” to carry their books and supplies instead of holding them in their arms the whole way.

During Hope & Care lessons

During Hope & Care activities, volunteers made simple paper-plate crafts with the children, sharing Jesus' love and the good news. It was joyful — but it sparked a deeper question: what could we create that is more durable and useful, something a child could truly keep and use in everyday life?

“Thank you for coming”

After one medical outreach, an elderly man received his medicine but didn't leave right away. He stayed to say, in halting French, “Thank you for coming to see us.” It showed Joanna that people need more than supplies and help — they need to know they are not forgotten.

Those three stories became one question in Joanna's heart:

What can I create to help these children?

That question became Pack with Love — a student-led, adult-guided service club where young people don't just watch others give, but use their own creativity, learning, and effort to make a useful, lasting, loving gift for another child.

Our purpose

Two hearts in one mission

From that one question grew a club with a double purpose — to create real help for children, and to help the young people who make it grow along the way.

Our Mission

To help young people use creativity, teamwork, and care to create practical and meaningful projects for children — in partnership with trusted local communities.

A Double Purpose

To care for children and communities, and to help young people grow through meaningful service. We don't measure success only by how many items we give.

Our foundation

Rooted in Christian love, compassion, and service

We believe every child is created with God-given dignity and deserves to be seen, valued, encouraged, and cared for. We seek to reflect the love of Jesus through joyful creation, thoughtful service, and practical care — always with humility, kindness, and respect.

We serve children and families of every background with respect. No child or family is ever required to attend an activity, share personal information, or take part in a follow-up program in order to receive a backpack or care item.

What we promise

The promises we keep for every project

  • Listen First

    We learn about the children and the setting before deciding what to create.

  • Create with Care

    We choose designs and items that are safe, useful, joyful, and culturally respectful.

  • Keep Children at the Center

    Children are people to love, never a photo opportunity.

  • Support Trusted Partners

    Our work strengthens the relationships local partners already have with families.

  • Grow Young Leaders

    Students carry real, age-appropriate responsibility; adults hold the guardrails.

  • Learn Before We Grow

    We reflect and improve before taking on bigger quantities or new partners.
