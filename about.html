with three stories

Pack with Love is an independent, youth-led service club — where creativity, teamwork, and compassion become real care for children and communities.

Our story

It began with a question

Founder Joanna Zhang grew up hearing her mom share experiences from mission trips to Africa. Three of them stayed with her — and slowly became the beginning of Pack with Love.

1
in the jungle of Djindjin

“A backpack.”

Many children walk hours to school each day, carrying their books and supplies in their arms the whole way. Asked what they needed most, they said food — which ACO already sends each year. Asked what else, they answered: “a backpack.”

2
during Hope & Care lessons

Something a child could keep

Volunteers made simple paper-plate crafts with the children, sharing Jesus' love and the good news. It was joyful — but it sparked a deeper question: what could we create that is durable and useful, something a child could truly keep and use in everyday life?

3
after a medical outreach

“Thank you for coming to see us”

An elderly man received his medicine but didn't leave right away. He stayed to say, in halting French, “Thank you for coming to see us.” People need more than supplies and help — they need to know they are not forgotten.

?
“What can I create to help these children?”

She kept turning that question over. Money alone couldn't answer it, and adults alone couldn't either — it called for something a kid could actually make: useful enough to carry every day, joyful enough to feel like play, personal enough to tell one child, you are not forgotten. The answer showed up close to home.

♥
at the kitchen table — the club is born

Two ideas became one

Joanna and her mom started brainstorming: what handcraft could VBS children keep playing with after the week ends — something more lasting than a paper-plate craft? Then the answer from Djindjin came back to them: “a backpack.” The two ideas merged into one — a color-it-yourself backpack, useful every day, joyful to finish, carrying its message for years. To build it, Joanna founded Pack with Love in June 2026, as a sixth grader — a student-led, adult-guided service club where young people don't just watch others give, but use their own creativity, learning, and effort to make a useful, lasting, loving gift for another child.

What we do

A backpack two children
finish together

Our signature gift starts as a theme design drawn by a student here — and becomes complete only when a child there colors it in and makes it their own. Something useful to carry. Something joyful to create. A message that stays.

1.

A kid designs it

Our student designers create the theme art — backpack panels and matching coloring books. drawn with love across the U.S.

2.

We raise it together

Gifts, kids' own fundraising claims, and a math class's sales — one shared goal turns the designs into real, produced, shipped backpacks. one goal, many hands

3.

A kid colors it

The child who receives it picks up the crayons and finishes the creation — their colors, their backpack, theirs to keep. And club members may travel with the mission to serve on site, handing the gift over in person. completed with joy in Senegal

4.

The message stays

Inside every set: a coloring book and words of encouragement that remind each child they are welcomed and valued. you are loved. you matter.

Our purpose

Two hearts in one mission

for the children who receive

The children our backpacks reach — like those across ten villages in Senegal. We create practical, meaningful, joyful gifts — in partnership with trusted local communities — so every one of them knows they are loved, seen, remembered, and that they matter.

for the children who make

Our club members — the students here at home. Here they learn real skills — design, research, planning, honest communication; grow through meaningful service, carrying real, age-appropriate responsibility in creativity, teamwork, and care; give — their creativity, their effort, and the funds they raise with their own hands; and step up to lead — projects, teams, and one another. We don't measure success only by how many items we give.

learn · grow · give · lead

Our foundation

Rooted in Christian love, compassion, and service

We believe every child is created with God-given dignity and deserves to be seen, valued, encouraged, and cared for. We seek to reflect the love of Jesus through joyful creation, thoughtful service, and practical care — always with humility, kindness, and respect.

We serve children and families of every background with respect.

No child is ever required to attend anything or share anything to receive a backpack. Every gift is unconditional.

The guardrails, plainly

Students lead in age-appropriate ways — vision, design, outreach, encouragement, and impact storytelling. Behind them stands a team of Adult Advisors — educators, a university professor, entrepreneurs, and a physician — who hold the responsibilities that should never rest on a child:

  • Money: all funds move through authorized adult-managed channels — students never collect, hold, or manage project money.
  • Safety: every in-person activity is supervised by an Adult Advisor; parent permission comes first.
  • Privacy: children's photos, names, and stories are shared only with partner approval and safeguarding rules.
  • Giving: no child is ever required to attend anything or share anything to receive a backpack.

“Adult Advisors build the guardrails; student leaders carry the vision forward.”
