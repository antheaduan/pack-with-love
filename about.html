who we are

A club that started
with three stories

Pack with Love is an independent, youth-led service club — where creativity, teamwork, and compassion become real care for children and communities.

Our story

It began with a question

Founder Joanna Zhang grew up hearing her mom share experiences from mission trips to Africa. Three of them stayed with her — and slowly became the beginning of Pack with Love.

1
in the jungle of Djindjin

“A backpack.”

Many children walk hours to school each day, carrying their books and supplies in their arms the whole way. Asked what they needed most, they said food — which ACO already sends each year. Asked what else, they answered: “a backpack.”

2
during Hope & Care lessons

Something a child could keep

Volunteers made simple paper-plate crafts with the children, sharing Jesus' love and the good news. It was joyful — but it sparked a deeper question: what could we create that is durable and useful, something a child could truly keep and use in everyday life?

3
after a medical outreach

“Thank you for coming to see us”

An elderly man received his medicine but didn't leave right away. He stayed to say, in halting French, “Thank you for coming to see us.” People need more than supplies and help — they need to know they are not forgotten.

?
“What can I create to help these children?”

That question became Pack with Love — a student-led, adult-guided service club where young people don't just watch others give, but use their own creativity, learning, and effort to make a useful, lasting, loving gift for another child.

Our purpose

Two hearts in one mission

for the children

To create practical, meaningful, joyful gifts for children — in partnership with trusted local communities — so every child knows they are loved, seen, remembered, and that they matter.

for the students

To help young people grow through meaningful service — carrying real, age-appropriate responsibility in creativity, teamwork, and care. We don't measure success only by how many items we give.

Our foundation

Rooted in Christian love, compassion, and service

We believe every child is created with God-given dignity and deserves to be seen, valued, encouraged, and cared for. We seek to reflect the love of Jesus through joyful creation, thoughtful service, and practical care — always with humility, kindness, and respect.

We serve children and families of every background with respect.

No child is ever required to attend anything or share anything to receive a backpack. Every gift is unconditional.
What we promise

The promises we keep for every project

Listen First

We learn about the children and the setting before deciding what to create.

Create with Care

We choose designs and items that are safe, useful, joyful, and culturally respectful.

Keep Children at the Center

Children are people to love, never a photo opportunity.

Support Trusted Partners

Our work strengthens the relationships local partners already have with families.

Grow Young Leaders

Students carry real, age-appropriate responsibility; adults hold the guardrails.

Learn Before We Grow

We reflect and improve before taking on bigger quantities or new partners.

The grown-ups

Adult Advisors build the guardrails

Money, contracts, safety, production, health, and partner commitments never rest on a child. Five advisors hold those responsibilities — and every one of them also teaches, mentoring students as they grow.

Lead Adult Advisor & Student Leadership Mentor

Xiaohong Duan

Overall supervision, student mentoring, safeguarding, parent communication, partnership coordination, and review of public messaging.

Also: lead teacher of the Data to Impact course.

Adult Finance Advisor & Financial Learning Mentor

Kevin Jialin Sun

Budgeting, financial literacy, fundraising transparency, cost planning, record review, and impact reporting — teaching students age-appropriate financial skills along the way.

Also: Associate Professor of Accounting, St. John's University · CFA — leads Session 1 of Data to Impact.

Production & Logistics Advisor · Project Operations Mentor

Jiangang (Tony) Yao

Supplier and factory communication, samples, purchasing, production, quality review, packing, and shipping — how creative ideas become practical products.

Also: nearly 30 years in international trade & supply chain — leads Session 2 of Data to Impact.

Overseas Operations Advisor & Business Mentor

Joyce Xie

Guidance on overseas production, quality, delivery, and how local markets really work — drawn from 20+ years running food manufacturing and wholesale businesses in Papua New Guinea.

Also: leads Session 3 of Data to Impact — landed cost in the real world.

Health & Safety Advisor · Service Trip Mentor

Dr. Christopher Leo, MD, PhD, FACP

Physician in Internal Medicine and Geriatrics and Assistant Professor at Duke University — guiding travel health, safety, and safeguarding for the club's service activities.

Also: leads pre-departure training — health, safety, conduct & cultural respect — for the Senegal journey.
